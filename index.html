How I work

Principles I bring to every engagement

Decide the irreversible things carefully

A wrong call on your data model or infrastructure at month three costs you a rewrite at month eighteen. I focus energy where reversing course is expensive.

Compliance is an architecture decision

Build controls into how teams already ship, give engineers clear guardrails, and make the audit trail a byproduct of good engineering.

AI as an operating layer, not a feature pile

One coherent framework, governed and secure by design, built on emerging protocols like MCP so you don't have to rebuild later.

Right-sized leadership

Most early startups don't need 40 hours a week of a CTO. They need 10 hours a week of the right one, and a team that can execute the rest.
